## Stage A: bounded independent sizing scouts
Start from exact F2. All W/L below in um, paired devices always symmetric. Baseline: NMOS M1/M2 50/40, PMOS M3/M4 8/42, MT 32/4, MC 10/4, MREF 2/4. Prior fixed-W mirror lengthening squeezed M4 headroom; constant W/L scaling may preserve inversion approximately, but check actual VDSAT, gm/Id, gm, gds, current, node voltages, poles and Cgg. Prior tail-current increase reduced input/thermal but increased mirror flicker; not assumed beneficial.
- N1 55/44 and N2 60/48 on input pair only.
- P1 10/52.5 and P2 12/63 on mirror only. If dimensions violate verified PDK/model range, skip and optionally use one smaller legal point such as 9/47.25, document. No unsupported model extrapolation.
- T1 MT 36/4 and T2 40/4 (other sizes baseline); if useful T0 MT 28/4 to examine noise/power tradeoff. Estimate static current from OP, not ideal ratios. Keep reference and other bias branches unchanged.
- Nominal DC+AC+noise across full band, CMRR modes, Zin and both STB for each valid point. Show individual noise source changes, especially input/mirror, not just total. Record all failures. Do not cherry-pick fixed dcc=1% to claim compliance.
- Choose up to 3 combinations of best viable N/P/T settings based on measured results. Favor area scaling at similar current before increasing current; prefer simpler, robust candidate. Limit Stage A incl baseline, scouts and combinations to <=11 configurations (one small legal fallback counts). No broad grid.
